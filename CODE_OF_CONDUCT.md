# Code of conduct

Thawr is maintained by one person in their spare time and used by people
who want their networks left alone. The same courtesy applies here.

## Expected

- Be precise and kind. Criticise code and ideas, never people.
- Assume good faith; ask before you conclude.
- Respect that "no" is a valid answer to a feature, and that the
  reasons are written down in `docs/VISION.md` and `docs/adr/`.
- Keep other people's networks, keys and addresses out of issues and
  pull requests.

## Not accepted

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, publishing
someone's private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@Sira-Labs](https://github.com/Sira-Labs))
handles reports sent by email (address on the profile) or through
GitHub's report feature, in confidence. Consequences range from a
warning to a permanent ban from the project's spaces, at the
maintainer's discretion.

This applies to every project space: issues, pull requests,
discussions, releases and any channel that represents the project.
